/* ___ Enemy_description_SLIME_OD ___ */

/* ___ Enemy_description_GOLEM ___ */
A social monster most often found near mining sites. They use their crafting skills to assemble dangerous contraptions. If you see one building something, take it out before it finishes.

/* ___ Enemy_description_GOLEM_OD ___ */
A monster whose size belies their equally high intelligence. Best to bait out their attacks, then dodge behind them before counterattacking.

/* ___ Enemy_description_MUSH ___ */
A spore-spouting monster often found in damp places. They retract underground when approached, but a good shotgun blast will still break through their defenses.

/* ___ Enemy_description_MUSH_OD ___ */
A spore-spouting monster that’s taking an opportunity to really stretch its legs. Its spore count will eventually drop and the clouds will become noticeably more spread out. Use that window to take it out.

/* ___ Enemy_description_PUPPY ___ */
A worm-shrouded monster that runs around at high speeds. The worms protect the monster from any incoming damage. Knock them off with a few quick hits, then go for the kill.

/* ___ Enemy_description_SPONGE ___ */
A monster that stores mana in orbs at the end of its stalks. Once the orbs are ready, it launches them at other monsters to restore their mana. Keep your distance once it starts thrashing about.

/* ___ Enemy_description_UNI ___ */
A monster that floats around lazily while launching spikes at its enemies. They can be a bit of a nuisance when fighting other monsters, so it's best to take them out first.

/* ___ Enemy_description_UNI_OD ___ */
A heavy monster that rolls around and sticks to walls and ceilings with its sharp spines. Stay far away when it lets out a howl so you don't get impaled.

/* ___ Enemy_description_SNAKE ___ */
A monster that crawls underground, then springs up beneath its victim and wraps them up. It can be stunned by tricking it into leaping up into a Ground Bomb.

/* ___ Enemy_description_GECKO ___ */
A monster that crawls around on all fours as quick as the wind. Give it a good smack when it jumps at you to knock it prone.

/* ___ Enemy_description_MAGE ___ */
A monster that only appears at night. It casts a replicated version of the spell “Lightning Bolt”. When targeted, they warp away to dodge the attack. Try to anticipate their movement and hit them hard and fast.

/* ___ Enemy_description_FOX ___ */
A rare monster that only appears in one spot in the forest. Their flames linger for a while, so be mindful of safe spots to keep fighting from. Their movement is limited to where mana plants grow.

/* ___ Enemy_description_FROG ___ */
A rare swamp-dwelling monster. A wily opportunist, it only attacks when it sees an opening. Turn your back to it to tempt it into attacking first, then hit it with a counter.

/* ___ Enemy_description_BOSS_NUSI ___ */
A large forest-dwelling monster.
