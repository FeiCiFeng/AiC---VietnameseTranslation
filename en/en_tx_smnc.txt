&&Smnc_td_rule_title1 Basics
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule1 ___ */
Use the provided funds to build walls and barriers
to slow the monster’s advance.

Space the walls apart (3 - 4 tiles) to allow room for cadets
to be stationed at each one to provide offensive countermeasures.


&&Smnc_td_rule_title2 Covering Fire
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule2 ___ */
Tigrina will provide long range cannon fire from the roof.

Her ammo is limited and must be purchased prior to the battle.
This is a good place to spend remaining funds once the
main defenses have been built.


&&Smnc_td_rule_title3 Covering Fire 2
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule3 ___ */
Tigrina has 4 different types of ammo available. Some directly damage monsters while others help set up opportunities for cadets to capitalize on.

Stay inside your defenses with your allies so you don't get caught in her crossfire.


&&Smnc_td_rule_title4 Deploying Cadets
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule4 ___ */
Place allies on the battlefield to assist in the fight. (Max &1 units)

Depending on where they are placed, they will automatically act as infantry,
support, or artillery.


&&Smnc_td_rule_title5 Infantry
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule5 ___ */
When placed at a barrier or watchtower, cadets will act as infantry and fire
White Arrows and Energy Balls at nearby monsters.

Cadets placed on watchtowers will prioritize casting Energy Ball.


&&Smnc_td_rule_title6 Infantry 2
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule6 ___ */
When their post is destroyed, infantry will retreat
and continue attacking from the rear.

Placing a Mana Silo within your defenses will help infantry units
defend their posts.


&&Smnc_td_rule_title7 Artillery
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule7 ___ */
Cadets placed on the roof with Tigrina will assist her with reloading,
allowing her to fire more rapidly.

Deploying too many cadets here will produce diminishing returns.


&&Smnc_td_rule_title8 Support
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule8 ___ */
Cadets placed near tents become support soldiers marked with a purple icon.
They will rescue injured infantry and get them back in the fight.

They work most efficiently when placed in pairs.


&&Smnc_td_rule_title9 Tents
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule9 ___ */
Fallen allies will recover within the tents and return to battle
after a short time.

If Primula is working in the tent, recovery speed will be increased.


&&Smnc_td_rule_title10 Tents 2
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule10 ___ */
The benefits of the recovery tent also extend to Noel while she's within the defensive lines.

Even if you’re knocked down, get back up and fight to the bitter end.


&&Smnc_td_rule_title11 For Your Safety
/* ___ Smnc_td_rule11 ___ */
Grenades are not allowed in this fight to prevent friendly fire accidents.

/* ___ Smnc_td_rule11_2 ___ */
All consumables will be returned to you after the battle is over.
