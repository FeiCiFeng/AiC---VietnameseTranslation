/* ___ tips_punch_0 ___ */
<key z/>Use your Weak Attack to knock mana out of a monster
for you to collect. You can also stun some monsters this way.

/* ___ tips_shotgun_0 ___ */
Press<key z/>while preparing to cast a spell to store it
in your staff. The stored spell can be instantly cast afterwards,
or used to transform any Weak Attack hit into a shotgun blast!

/* ___ tips_shotgun_1 ___ */
Shotgun attacks have different strength and effects depending on
the spell that was used. A stored Ground Bomb, for example,
has an extremely high chance of inflicting Stun.

/* ___ tips_evade ___ */
Dodge often (<key lsh/>+<key la/>or<key ra/>) to keep out of harm's way.

/* ___ tips_shield ___ */
Your shield (<key lsh/>) protects you from drowning
and the effects of toxic gas while up.

/* ___ tips_sliding ___ */
Slide with <key ba/>+<key z/>. Be careful not to hit
enemies with it, or you’ll lose your balance.

/* ___ tips_magic_damage ___ */
Enemies take greatly reduced damage from
magic attacks while off-screen.

/* ___ tips_noon_night ___ */
Different enemies start to appear as day turns to night.
Weaker ones begin to swarm, and powerful, unique enemies
will aggressively ambush passersby.

/* ___ tips_treasure_0 ___ */
The amount and grade of items from treasure chests are
affected by reel modifiers. Hold off on opening them while
gathering reel modifiers to increase the reward potential.

/* ___ tips_treasure_1 ___ */
When Noel is defeated, all held treasure chests are
opened immediately with no reel modifiers applied.

/* ___ tips_treasure_2 ___ */
Defeat contaminated enemy variants for a
shot at obtaining special materials.

/* ___ tips_battle_nusi_1 ___ */
Repel the Lord of the Forest's seeds and thorn
bushes with a weak attack or Shield Bash.

/* ___ tips_nusi_shield_bush_0 ___ */
Repel incoming projectiles with a Shield Bash(<key lsh/>+<key z/>).

/* ___ tips_grazia_omorashi ___ */
If the bartender really is a monster, he shouldn’t
be able to resist elven fluids. It would be
ill-mannered to spray some in the bar, but...

/* ___ tips_noel_egg ___ */
To collect monster eggs, they must be removed
while out of combat. Rumor has it the merchant
has an item to help collect them more safely.

/* ___ tips_nightingale_bell ___ */
Waiting at a bench while you have the "Merchant’s Bell"
will increase the likelihood of the merchant appearing.

/* ___ tips_granade_0 ___ */
If you’re holding more than four grenades,
they may self-destruct when you take fire damage.

// ヘビ戦
/* ___ bosshint_102_0 ___ */
Drop a Ground Bomb beneath your feet and trick
the Molesnake into eating it, then attack it
while it's disoriented.

/* ___ bosshint_102_1 ___ */
If Noel is directly on top of a Ground Bomb
when the Molesnake emerges, priority is given
to the Ground Bomb.

Standing directly on top of your own bomb is
the safest place to be.


// ヌシ戦
/* ___ bosshint_104_0 ___ */
A lot of the Lord of the Forest's attacks leave
behind gas, spikes, and fire, limiting safe
spaces to stand while fighting.

Use ranged attacks, such as <font color="ff:#ffff33">Shield Bash</font>
to knock the spores, bushes, and grenades it drops before they
detonate to keep the area clear.

/* ___ bosshint_104_1 ___ */
If you're on fire, use your Burst<key PR_BURST/> skill
to clear the negative effects. This is risky if you do
it too often, but it might be better than running into
spikes or other hazards.


// ヌシ戦
/* ___ bosshint_104_noburst_0 ___ */
Ixia seems to have dropped something important.
Use<key a/>to locate it.

/* ___ bosshint_107_0 ___ */
Use the "Auto-build" function if you're not sure
where to start. You can edit the resulting loadout afterwards.

/* ___ bosshint_107_1 ___ */
Place a few cadets up in Tigrina's tower to help
keep things smooth during battle.

Cadets placed up there will help her reload,
allowing her to fire more rapidly. This has
diminishing returns though, so don't place too many.
